How much does Sealed hide?

This page runs Sealed's own sealing code on invented Indian medical reports where every identifier is known, and counts what is still readable. It runs in your browser and sends nothing to any cloud AI.

An identifier counts as hidden only if none of its own words or numbers can still be read anywhere in the report. Common words such as "Road" or "Hospital" do not count. A result counts as kept only if its whole line is unchanged.

How to read this

  • The unseen score is the honest one. Gemini wrote those 20 reports after the rules were frozen. We added marks it had missed and replaced names of real organisations. We did not change the rules afterwards.
  • The limits: 20 reports is a small set, and they were written by a Gemini model, so real reports will differ. A small model can also hide a little too much, which is counted above.
  • Pattern rules are good at numbers, phone numbers and labelled fields. Names in the middle of a sentence are what the on-device model is for.
  • No automatic sealing is perfect. That is why Sealed always shows you the sealed text and lets you hide more before anything is sent.
  • Every person, place, organisation and result here is invented.
