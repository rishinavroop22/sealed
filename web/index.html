Understand your medical report.
Keep your name out of it.

Sealed reads your report with an AI model that runs inside this browser, removes everything that identifies you, and shows you exactly what is left. Only that sealed version is ever sent to the cloud for a full explanation, and you can choose to keep even that on your device.

  • Sealed on your device. The original never leaves this browser.
  • You see what leaves. Every cloud request is shown word for word.
  • Works offline. No signal? The on-device model explains it instead.

Use ChatGPT or Gemini? The Sealed Chrome extension seals a report on your device when you paste it into any chatbot. Try it on the test page.
