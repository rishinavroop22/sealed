How to turn on on-device AI in Chrome
  1. Use Chrome 139 or later on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer (not a phone). The computer needs about 22 GB of free disk space.
  2. Open chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano-multimodal-input, set it to Enabled, and relaunch Chrome.
  3. Come back here and click Try with a sample report. The first run downloads Gemini Nano, which can take a few minutes.

Without on-device AI, Sealed still works: it seals with pattern rules only, says so clearly, and asks you to check the result before anything is sent.
